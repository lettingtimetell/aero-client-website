About Aero Client

What is Aero Client?

A client for Minecraft: Java Edition 1.8.9 multiplayer. You use it instead of the original 1.8.9 game program to play on 1.8.9 servers. It's written in Rust, runs on a modern GPU renderer, and has the mods PvP players use built in.

Is it free?

Yes. There are no ads, no paid features and no Aero Client account.

Is it made by Mojang or Microsoft?

No. Aero Client is an independent project. It is not an official Minecraft product, and it is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

Does it include Minecraft?

No. Aero Client contains none of Minecraft's game files and none of its source code. You need your own copy of Minecraft: Java Edition, and Aero Client uses the 1.8.9 files from it. To get them, start version 1.8.9 once from the official Minecraft Launcher.

Which versions does it play?

1.8.9, multiplayer only. It joins servers that accept 1.8.9 clients, including servers that accept several versions. There are no singleplayer worlds.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, with a graphics card that supports Vulkan or DirectX 12. Other systems may come later.

Can I add other mods or resource packs?

Resource packs work as they do in 1.8.9, including the ones servers offer. Aero Client doesn't load Java mods: its mods are built in, and you switch them on and off in the mod menu (Right Shift).

Your account and your data

Why do I have to sign in with Microsoft?

Servers in online mode ask Mojang to confirm who you are and that you own the game. To answer, the client needs a Minecraft access token, and that comes from signing in with the Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition.

How do I sign in?

In Aero Client, open the Accounts screen (the account corner at the top right of the title screen), choose Add account, then Sign in with Microsoft. The client shows a short code: choose Open page, enter the code on Microsoft's page and sign in there.

Can Aero Client see my password?

No. You sign in on Microsoft's own website with a one-time code that the client shows you. Aero Client only receives the tokens Microsoft gives it afterwards.

Does Aero Client send my data anywhere?

Only where playing needs it: to Microsoft and Xbox Live to sign you in, to Mojang to check your account and your server joins, and to the servers you join or keep in your server list. There's no telemetry, analytics or advertising, and Aero Client has no server of its own. The one server of ours it can talk to is Mega Skywars, a Minecraft server that new players find in their server list; remove it if you don't want it. The full list is in the privacy policy.

Can I play without owning Minecraft, or offline?

No. Aero Client checks that your account owns Minecraft: Java Edition when you sign in, and public builds have no offline mode.

How do I sign out and delete my data?

Remove the account on the Accounts screen to delete its saved sign-in. To remove everything, uninstall Aero Client (see below), then also delete the %APPDATA%\mc-rs folder and the %LOCALAPPDATA%\Aero Client folder, where Aero Client remembers where your Minecraft files are. You can also withdraw Aero Client's permission from your Microsoft account at account.live.com/consent/Manage: choose Aero Client, then remove its permissions.

How do I uninstall it?

If you used the installer: open Settings, then Apps, then Installed apps, find Aero Client and choose Uninstall. It asks whether to delete your settings, screenshots and replays too; say no to keep them for later.

If you use the portable version: delete its folder.

Either way, your saved sign-ins stay in %APPDATA%\mc-rs until you remove the accounts on the Accounts screen or delete that folder.

What about child accounts and family settings?

They apply as usual. If an account isn't allowed to play online, Microsoft or Mojang refuse the sign-in or the server join, and Aero Client shows their message. It never tries to get around these settings.

Playing

Can I get banned for using Aero Client?

We can't rule it out. Aero Client has no cheats: nothing aims, clicks or fights for you, and nothing shows what the game hides. But it is experimental: we aim to make it behave exactly like the original 1.8.9 client, and we can't guarantee that it does in every case. Every server decides which clients and mods it allows, and some check how clients behave. Use your own discretion: read a server's rules before you play, and switch off any mod it doesn't allow. You use Aero Client on servers at your own risk, and we are not responsible for bans.

Does it look and play like the original?

That's the aim. With every mod off, Aero Client is built to have the same movement, the same packets in the same order, and the same visuals as 1.8.9, and parity tests compare it with the original game's behavior. Tests can't cover every situation, though, so we can't guarantee that it matches in every case. The mods only change what they're switched on for, and a few start switched on (the mod menu marks them "On by default in Aero"); switch any of them off to get the original behavior. When you join a server, Aero Client tells it that it is Aero Client, where the original client says "vanilla" (the privacy policy says exactly what is sent).

Where do my replays and videos go?

Recordings stay on your PC, and exported videos are saved in your Videos folder, under Aero Client. Nothing is uploaded. A recording contains what you saw while it ran, including other players' names and chat, so think before you share one.

The project

Where do I download it?

From the Download button on the home page. Before anything downloads, it reminds you that Aero Client is experimental, that we can't guarantee it behaves exactly like the original game, and that you use it on servers at your own discretion and risk. Then choose a version:

  • Installer: installs Aero Client for your Windows account, in a folder you can choose, with shortcuts on the desktop and in the Start menu. It doesn't need administrator rights.
  • Portable: a zip you unpack into any folder, even a USB stick, and start from there. Nothing is installed.

Both are the same client, both always come in the latest version, and both tell you when an update is out.

Windows says it protected my PC. Is Aero Client safe?

Windows SmartScreen warns about programs it hasn't seen many people download yet, and Aero Client is new, so you may see "Windows protected your PC" the first time. If you downloaded it from this website or from our GitHub page, choose More info, then Run anyway. Its whole source code is public there, so anyone can check what it does. If your antivirus flags it, please tell us at pvp@pvp.dog.

Where is the source code?

On GitHub: github.com/lettingtimetell/aero-client. Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later.

Who makes Aero Client?

Aero Client is made by pvps and contributors: it's open source, and anyone can contribute on GitHub. Write to pvp@pvp.dog.

How do I report a bug or a security problem?

Bugs: open an issue on GitHub. Security problems, especially anything about sign-in: please email pvp@pvp.dog privately instead of opening a public issue.
